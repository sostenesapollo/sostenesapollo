### Numbers I keep looking at

- 38% of all digital gifts are birthdays. 39% are romantic. There is no third category.
- Peak creation time is Saturday, 15h UTC — midday in Brazil. Not midnight panic.
- 98% of people never pay. For a one-shot emotional product, that's the correct number, not a failure.
